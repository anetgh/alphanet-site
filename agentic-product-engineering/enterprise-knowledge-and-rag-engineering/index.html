By centralizing data and automating workflows, teams collaborate in real time, eliminate manual handoffs, and maintain compliance at every stage.

Strengths

Six core capabilities that set our PLM solution apart from the competition

Explore Our Capabilities

Unified Data & Traceability

All requirements, CAD models, bills of materials (BOMs), and change requests live in one place, ensuring end-to-end visibility from concept through retirement.

Deep CAD & ERP Integration

Native connectors for SolidWorks™, PTC Creo™, Siemens NX™, SAP, Oracle, and Microsoft Dynamics keep design updates and BOM changes flowing downstream without delay.

Fast Change Control

Engineering Change Orders (ECOs) can be created, routed, and approved in hours—not weeks—thanks to automated workflows and built-in audit trails

Built-In Quality & Compliance

Embedded FMEA, CAPA, and 8D checks plus electronic signatures and version control keep you audit-ready for ISO, FDA 21 CFR Part 11, ITAR, and other standards.

Secure Supplier Collaboration

Share approved drawings, specifications, and revisions via a secure portal. Suppliers upload compliance documents and purchase-ready files directly into PLM.

Real-Time Analytics

Dashboards track ECO cycle time, BOM accuracy, and compliance metrics—turning complex data into actionable insights.
